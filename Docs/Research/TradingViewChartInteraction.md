# TradingView chart interaction reference

Research date: 2026-10-07. This note is a product-interaction reference, not a decision to embed TradingView, copy its visuals, or change this project's market-data semantics.

## First-party observations

- A chart resolution is the duration represented by one bar; TradingView lists `60` minutes as one hour and `1D` as one day. Its horizontal time scale displays bar times, while the vertical price scale maps prices to coordinates. The visible time range changes through scaling and scrolling. [Resolution](https://www.tradingview.com/charting-library-docs/latest/core_concepts/Resolution/), [Time scale](https://www.tradingview.com/charting-library-docs/latest/ui_elements/Time-Scale/), [Price scale](https://www.tradingview.com/charting-library-docs/latest/ui_elements/Price-Scale/).
- TradingView's volume indicator uses the volume associated with each main-series bar at that same time; its documentation explicitly describes one volume bar per weekly price bar. The product supports moving indicators to the main pane or another pane. Thus a separately scaled volume region does not require a separate, unrelated time axis. [Extending the time scale](https://www.tradingview.com/charting-library-docs/latest/custom_studies/Studies-Extending-The-Time-Scale/), [Indicator placement](https://www.tradingview.com/charting-library-docs/latest/ui_elements/indicators/indicator-placement/), [Move an indicator to the main pane](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000762829-how-to-move-an-indicator-to-the-main-pane/).
- Navigation controls support zoom in/out, horizontal scrolling, and reset. The Advanced Charts shortcut reference additionally documents focused-area zoom with Ctrl + mouse wheel and horizontal movement with Shift + mouse wheel. Pane controls can collapse or maximize a pane. [Configure Supercharts](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000748166-how-to-configure-your-supercharts/), [Keyboard shortcuts](https://www.tradingview.com/charting-library-docs/latest/getting_started/Shortcuts/).
- A visible time range is a viewport, not a data-selection limit. TradingView notes that very dense ranges may require smaller bar spacing and that this may cost calculation time and memory. [Time scale](https://www.tradingview.com/charting-library-docs/latest/ui_elements/Time-Scale/).
- In Bar Replay, the user chooses a starting bar on the chart; Play advances bars, and speed can change before or during playback. TradingView's separate historical-trading feature can show executions as arrows with quantity and price near the chart; its chart settings describe execution arrows and labels. [Bar Replay](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000474024-how-do-i-turn-bar-replay-on/), [Historical trading](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000691889-learn-to-trade-on-historical-data/), [Configure Supercharts](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000748166-how-to-configure-your-supercharts/).
- TradingView warns that incorporating far-away indicator levels into automatic price scaling can visually compress candles; it offers a price-only scale option. This is relevant to keeping volume and buy/sell glyph bounds out of the candle price scale. [Chart compression](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000762934-my-chart-looks-compressed/).

## Design inferences for this project (not TradingView requirements)

- Present Replay as one cohesive chart surface: price candles in the large upper region, volume bars in a compact lower region, and one visually continuous, synchronized time axis. Retain separate numerical scales for price and volume. This addresses the reported appearance of two disconnected charts while preserving accurate units.
- Make horizontal bar-spacing zoom a first-class mouse-wheel and button interaction. Keep zoom and pan independent of the immutable full selected-bar timeline and replay cursor; changing the viewport must neither erase loaded bars nor expose future bars ahead of replay.
- Auto-fit the **visible candles'** high/low with appropriate margins, excluding volume values and execution glyphs from price-scale bounds. Make the lower volume region proportionally smaller but legible; a reasonable initial ratio must be checked in the actual app at multiple window sizes, not assumed from this research.
- Anchor buy/sell markers to the exact containing candle's horizontal bar coordinate and its relevant price, and reveal them only when replay reaches their persisted fill bar. The cited TradingView execution style is a behavioral reference, not a mandate to reproduce its colors, icons, or branding.

## Limits

The cited Advanced Charts documentation describes TradingView's library, and Supercharts support pages describe its product UI. Exact gestures can differ between products, platforms, and user settings. This note does not assert that TradingView uses a single canvas/widget internally or that its implementation can be copied into this Qt project.
